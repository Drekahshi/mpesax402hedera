01 — Architecture Overview

A multi-chain framework, built for two different jobs.

KAI runs entirely on Ethereum & Hedera because one chain already does both jobs well. Sub-second finality and near-zero fees make it viable for cents-denominated savings, insurance premiums, and pension top-ups moving constantly in the background, while deep DeFi liquidity and full EVM composability make it the venue for yield strategy execution. Each of the six tokens keeps a role that stays compartmentalized rather than overlapping — governance doesn't touch the buffer layer, the buffer layer doesn't touch rewards, and rewards never carry investment risk.

Governance & Protocol Utility
Governance · Revenue Share

Specification

Max Supply
50,000,000 NVR
Chain Split
Ethereum & Hedera

Architecture & Mechanics

  • Cross-chain governance — coordinated via a shared governance module using cross-chain message passing between Ethereum & Hedera, so a single vote carries weight on both deployments.
  • Protocol control — NVR holders vote on protocol parameters, vault strategy approvals, fee structures, risk parameters, and treasury allocations.
  • Revenue distribution — protocol revenue from vault performance fees, escrow, and transaction fees flows to staked NVR holders.
  • Deflationary mechanism — periodic buyback-and-burn actions reduce circulating supply over time.

Architecture & Mechanics

  • Drawdown absorption — sits between the yield vaults and every downstream payout, so a bad week in the market shows up as a smaller buffer, not a missed insurance premium or pension distribution.
  • Vault-linked yield — earns a steady 7.5% vault APY while it sits as a buffer, so protection doesn't come at the cost of idle capital.
