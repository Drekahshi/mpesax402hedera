KAI Ecosystem · Retirement Platform

Build wealth while you work.

KAI Pension is a digital retirement wealth platform that turns contributions into productive capital — combining intelligent investing, AI-powered planning, insurance integration, and estate planning into one lifelong financial journey.

"Retire with confidence. Leave a lasting legacy."

01

Overview

KAI Pension helps individuals, employers, businesses, SACCOs, and communities build long-term retirement wealth through productive capital. Rather than simply saving money, users invest in approved KAI ecosystem products, while KAI partners with licensed pension providers and retirement administrators where required by law.

02

Our vision

Retirement should be a time of financial independence, not financial uncertainty. KAI Pension empowers people to build sustainable retirement income while protecting and growing their wealth throughout every stage of life.

03

How it works

A lifelong journey — from your first contribution to the legacy you leave behind.

1

Create your retirement plan

Select your retirement goals, preferred retirement age, contribution schedule, and risk profile. KAI AI helps design a personalized strategy around your objectives.

2

Make contributions

Contribute regularly or make one-time deposits, from any of these sources:

  • Individuals
  • Employers
  • SACCOs
  • Chamas
  • Businesses
  • Family members
  • Diaspora supporters
3

Build productive capital

Contributions are invested into approved KAI investment strategies for long-term growth. Assets remain invested, letting returns compound as retirement wealth builds.

4

Pause, resume & rebalance

Life circumstances change. KAI lets users pause and resume contributions without closing their plan. During reduced contributions, AI reviews the portfolio and recommends adjustments to stay aligned with long-term goals.

5

Protect your retirement

Integrate KAI Insurance to help protect the retirement journey. Eligible investment returns can help fund insurance premiums, easing financial pressure while preserving long-term goals.

Links to KAI Insurance
6

Retire your way

Choose how to receive retirement benefits, with AI-guided withdrawal recommendations designed to help savings last longer.

  • Monthly income
  • Quarterly income
  • Annual withdrawals
  • Flexible drawdowns
  • Continued investment with partial withdrawals
7

Leave a legacy

If retirement assets remain after the user's lifetime, they can transfer seamlessly into a KAI Trust — continuing to support spouses, children, charities, or future generations.

Links to KAI Trust

Trust & compliance

KAI Pension is not a pension regulator or pension fund manager. KAI works alongside licensed pension providers, retirement administrators, custodians, and financial institutions where required by applicable laws — providing the digital infrastructure that helps users manage retirement wealth more intelligently while maintaining compliance with local regulations.
