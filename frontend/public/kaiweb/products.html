03 · Payment Infrastructure

How value actually moves

Three pieces of rail connect a stated financial goal to real settlement, whether the counterparty lives on-chain, in a bank, or on M-Pesa.

APaaS

Autonomous Payments as a Service

Users define outcomes, not instructions — "pay my insurance premium," "save 10% of every payment received," "settle my AI agent's bills." The protocol executes the rule automatically.

User-defined rule
↓
Automation engine
↓
Settled obligation
Bi-Directional Gateway

M-Pesa ⇄ Nuvari

On-ramp: M-Pesa funds convert into ETH, NUVARI STABLE, or ETFs for savings and investment. Off-ramp: investment returns and pension payouts convert back to KES on M-Pesa.

M-Pesa → Gateway → On-chain assets
Returns → Gateway → KES → M-Pesa
x402

Machine-to-Machine Settlement

Programmable settlement between Nuvari and external services — insurers, AI agents, merchant APIs, data providers. x402-enabled counterparties settle directly; others route through Nuvari's gateway to M-Pesa or bank rails, with a full on-chain audit trail either way.

Yield generated → x402 settlement → API / Gateway → M-Pesa or Bank

Architecture Highlights

Why it's built this way

A

Insurance funded by yield, not income

Yield-as-Insurance turns a recurring expense into a byproduct of capital already working — coverage grows as yield accrues instead of competing with monthly cash flow.

B

A stablecoin that's actually stable

NUVARI STABLE is 150% over-collateralized in USDC, not algorithmic — the ecosystem's settlement layer doesn't inherit the fragility that sank earlier stablecoin designs.

C

Settlement that degrades gracefully

x402-enabled counterparties settle directly on-chain; everyone else still gets paid through the M-Pesa or bank gateway — the audit trail never breaks, even off-chain.
