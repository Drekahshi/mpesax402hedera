Protect your wealth. Grow your legacy.

KAI Trust digitizes trust administration — combining traditional trust principles with blockchain transparency and AI assistance, so families can preserve, grow, and transfer wealth across generations from a single platform.

"Pass it on with confidence."

01

Overview

KAI Trust is a digital family office for individuals, families, businesses, and communities. It manages investments, insurance, inheritance, education funds, retirement savings, and estate planning from one platform — digitizing trust administration through automation, AI assistance, and blockchain transparency, while partnering with licensed trustees where required by law. KAI Trust does not replace legal trust institutions.

02

Our vision

Wealth should outlive its creator. Too often, families lose wealth to poor planning, lengthy probate, legal disputes, inflation, or fragmented financial management. KAI Trust exists to simplify wealth management and make professional trust services accessible to everyone — not just the wealthy.

03

What is a KAI Trust?

A legally structured digital trust where a Trustor (Settlor) transfers selected assets into a trust managed according to predetermined instructions. Assets continue to be professionally managed while following the trustor's instructions.

Digital assets Investment portfolios Tokenized real-world assets Education funds Retirement savings Insurance funding accounts Business succession plans Property ownership records Family wealth reserves

05

How it works

From creating the trust to eventual distribution — a defined sequence, verified at every step.

1

Create your trust

Use guided AI-assisted questionnaires or professionally designed templates.

  • Family Trust
  • Education Trust
  • Retirement Trust
  • Business Trust
  • Charitable Trust
  • Legacy Trust
2

Define your instructions

The Trustor specifies the terms that become part of the trust deed.

  • Beneficiaries
  • Distribution percentages
  • Trigger events
  • Investment objectives
  • Insurance preferences
  • Trustees
  • Successor trustees
  • Optional guardians or enforcers
3

Fund the trust

Assets are transferred into the trust — KAI ecosystem assets, stable assets, investment portfolios, tokenized assets, cash equivalents, or insurance reserves.

4

Wealth management

The trust operates according to its objectives, with KAI providing portfolio monitoring, AI financial insights, insurance integration, performance reporting, and automated administration. Where required by law, licensed trustees maintain fiduciary oversight.

5

Distribution

When predefined conditions occur — retirement, graduation, death, business succession, specific dates, or medical events — the trust executes the instructions in the trust deed after appropriate verification.

06

Roles within a KAI Trust

Trustor
Settlor

The individual or organization creating the trust.

  • Defining beneficiaries
  • Funding the trust
  • Establishing trust rules
  • Choosing trustees
Trustee

Manages the trust according to the trust deed. Depending on jurisdiction, this may be a licensed trust company, a regulated financial institution, or an approved fiduciary partner. KAI provides technology and administration but does not replace regulated trustees where legally required.

Beneficiaries

The people or organizations entitled to benefit from the trust — family members, children, businesses, charities, or community organizations.

Enforcer
Optional

An independent role some trusts appoint to ensure the trustee follows the trust deed — particularly useful for purpose trusts and complex family structures.
