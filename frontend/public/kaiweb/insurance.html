02

What KAI is — and isn't

KAI is financial infrastructure, not an insurer. We connect DeFi investment vaults, automated payments, licensed insurance providers, digital identity, and AI-driven recommendations — making insurance smarter, not different.

KAI is not an insurance company.
KAI does not underwrite risk.

03

How it works

A real sequence, from choosing coverage to building capital that eventually pays for itself.

1

Choose your insurance provider

Connect an existing policy or purchase a new one through a licensed partner. The insurance contract always remains between you and the licensed provider.

  • Health · Motor · Business · Property · Life · Agricultural
2

Build productive capital

Instead of paying premiums directly, stake assets inside the KAI ecosystem — YToken, YBOB, stable assets, liquidity positions, or other approved products. These assets continue to belong to you.

3

Risk assessment

KAI's Risk Engine estimates the productive capital required to sustainably support your premiums, scaled to the risk profile of your chosen coverage.

4

Earn investment returns

Staked assets generate rewards based on your strategy and market conditions. These returns belong to you, and can be allocated toward funding your premiums instead of withdrawn.

5

Automatic premium payments

KAI integrates automated payment infrastructure — including the x402 payment protocol — to settle premiums directly with your licensed provider. KAI monitors schedules, calculates available rewards, pays when funds are sufficient, and records every payment transparently.

6

Continue building wealth

As productive assets grow, more rewards are generated, larger portions of premiums are covered automatically, and additional insurance products become accessible.

07

Withdrawing productive assets

Customers retain ownership of everything deposited into the KAI ecosystem, and may withdraw principal at any time — subject to the terms of the selected insurance product.

Withdrawing productive assets may reduce or eliminate the investment returns funding your premiums. If a withdrawal takes your portfolio below the minimum capital required for an active policy:

  1. Future automatic premium payments may be paused.
  2. You'll be notified before any scheduled payment is missed.
  3. You can top up your portfolio or make a manual payment to keep coverage uninterrupted.
  4. If minimum capital isn't restored, KAI's premium payment service may be suspended until compliance is restored.
KAI does not seize customer assets. You always retain ownership of your principal — continued participation in the premium-funding program simply requires meeting the agreed terms. If available rewards fall short of a scheduled premium, KAI notifies you in advance and rewards keep accumulating toward the next one.
08

Compliance

KAI operates as financial infrastructure and insurance payment automation. Insurance policies remain under licensed insurance providers in their respective jurisdictions. KAI does not assume insurance risk or replace regulated insurance companies. Returns are not guaranteed and depend on the performance of underlying assets and prevailing market conditions.
